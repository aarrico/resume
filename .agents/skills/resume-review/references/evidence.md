# Evidence and limits

Sources reviewed 2026-10-15. These inform review heuristics; they do not validate
this skill or establish causal effects on interview rates.

## Employer guidance

[Amazon recruiter advice](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-job-application-resume-writing-tips)
recommends simple presentation, relevant accomplishments, and actions with results.
It encourages measurement where available and acknowledges that some accomplishments
have no numerical measurement. Apply this as employer guidance, not a universal rubric.

## Parser behavior

[Greenhouse: unsuccessful resume parse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse)
documents potential problems with image resumes, columns, complex formatting, and
contact information in headers or text boxes. It describes parsing as populating
candidate fields; failed extraction can require manual correction.

Use this to motivate checking reading order and field associations. It does not
establish an automatic rejection rate or a score shared across applicant systems.
Preserve real company names even when a parser's advice suggests corporate suffixes.

## Practitioner guidance and disagreements

[Tech Interview Handbook's resume guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/)
is written from software engineering interviewing experience. Useful themes include
prioritizing relevant achievements, clear experience structure, and avoiding keyword
stuffing. Its blanket recommendations for one page and Word/Google Docs are not
requirements of this skill; evaluate the actual artifact and candidate's context.
Its ATS guarantees and vendor promotions are not evidence of parser compatibility.

[The Tech Resume Inside Out](https://thetechresume.com/) identifies engineering
managers and recruiters as contributors and describes the space-versus-focus tradeoff
for experienced candidates. The public page supports its practitioner provenance;
it is not an independent evaluation. The full paid book was not reviewed.

## Design judgments to validate through use

Evidence mappings, explicit factual dependencies, technical-mechanism checks, and
the review-first default are workflow choices for this skill. Assess them by whether
reviews surface important issues, preserve facts, and produce useful revisions.
Measure interview conversion only through a user's actual application history with
appropriate context; a cleaner review alone cannot establish a causal improvement.
